ABOUT PARAGRAPH:
SPE Suez University Student Chapter is the largest SPE student chapter in the MENA region, founded in 2004 and actively operating since 2008. Operating under the Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE), the chapter is dedicated to empowering students and fresh graduates through a comprehensive blend of technical education, professional development, leadership training, and community engagement.

The chapter organizes certified courses, technical workshops, engineering programs, competitions, research activities, field visits, and international conference participation, ensuring strong alignment between academic learning and industry needs. SPE Suez has achieved outstanding international recognition, earning multiple Presidential Awards for Outstanding Student Chapter and consistently ranking among the top student chapters worldwide. Alongside technical excellence, the chapter actively promotes sustainability, innovation, social responsibility, and diversity.
