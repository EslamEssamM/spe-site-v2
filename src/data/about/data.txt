Who Are We
SPE Suez University Student Chapter is the largest SPE student chapter in the MENA region. It was established in 2004 and has been actively operating since 2008. The chapter is dedicated to empowering students through a wide range of technical, professional, and developmental activities. 
Our mission is to prepare students and fresh graduates academically and professionally through hands-on experience, global exposure, and strong industry connections.
What We Do
SPE Suez organizes technical workshops, certified courses, internships, and engineering sessions across multiple disciplines. The chapter conducts field visits and facilitates direct industry interaction to bridge the gap between academia and real-world practice. In addition, we build strong connections with major national and international companies while focusing on developing leadership, innovation, teamwork, and professional skills among students.
Awards & Recognition
SPE Suez has received the Presidential Award for Outstanding Student Chapter in the years 2014, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2020, 2022, and 2025.
The chapter also achieved the Gold Standard Award in 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, and 2016.
Presidential Award for Outstanding Student Chapter (2025)
SPE Suez achieved the Presidential Award for Outstanding Student Chapter in the field of technical dissemination and professional development for the seventh time. This achievement reaffirmed the chapter’s position among the top three performing student chapters worldwide. After passing both regional and global evaluation rounds, SPE Suez ranked within the top three chapters globally and was recognized at ATCE as one of the most impactful student chapters internationally.

3.Petroanalyst Competition
The PetroAnalyst Competition is a hands-on technical initiative that trains students to transform petroleum data into engineering decisions using Python. Participants develop a Streamlit-based application focused on production-related analysis, combining data analysis skills with petroleum engineering knowledge.
Evaluation is based on completing core analytical tasks, optional advanced challenges, and a short technical presentation explaining the methodology and insights. The competition targets students with basic Python and data analysis backgrounds, preparing them for data-driven roles in the petroleum industry.
